Public URL (once the LB + cert provision):
  https://{{ .Values.ingress.host }}

The HTTPS Load Balancer takes 5-10 minutes to come up. The Google
managed certificate then transitions FailedNotVisible -> Provisioning
-> Active over 15-60 minutes for the first issuance. FailedNotVisible
for the first ~30 min is expected while DNS propagation completes.

Check progress:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get ingress {{ .Release.Name }}-bank-app
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get managedcertificate {{ .Release.Name }}-cert \
    -o jsonpath='{.status.certificateStatus}{"\n"}'

Registrar delegation (once-only):
The Cloud DNS managed zone is provisioned by Crossplane on the xp-mp
management cluster. Read its four nameservers and paste them as NS
records for the `queenswood` subdomain at your registrar:

  just gcp-dns-ns

If the cert sticks on FailedNotVisible beyond ~30 min, the NS
delegation at the registrar is the most likely culprit -- re-check
that all four ns-cloud-cN.googledomains.com. values are set
correctly.
